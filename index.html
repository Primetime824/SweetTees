Welcome to Sweet T's Shear Genius

With 18 years of experience as a barber, my career began in my teenage years and has been marked by a dedication to mastering the craft. I studied at Virginia College in Birmingham, Alabama, where I was fortunate to be mentored by esteemed professionals such as Toni Love, Annette Bigham, and Queen Esther. Over the years, I have held managerial positions at Sport Clips, Supercuts, and Great Clips, where I also had the opportunity to mentor numerous stylists. To stay at the forefront of industry trends and techniques, I have attended various seminars and classes across the United States. My clientele is diverse, encompassing high-profile coaches, social media icons, professionals, college students, and children of all ages.
